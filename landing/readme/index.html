Hold HOME for a second, then…

The grown-ups' settings all start the same way. Hold HOME for 1 second and keep holding it, then:

ThenWhat it does
KNOB 4The hat. Ten hats any friend can wear (party, crown, top hat, rainbow bucket, wizard, beanie, western, chef, propeller, flowers), or none. The band names it. It stays on after power-off. Ten stars add an eleventh, the STAR hat.
OCT- / OCT+Volume. The most the MASTER knob can give, in eight steps. It starts at step 6, about half. Step 8 lets MASTER go all the way.
ALGORITHMThe name in the hello. PRESETS picks the letter, ALGORITHM moves to the next place, SAVE keeps it. HOME leaves without changing anything.
KNOB 3Stars. How close the next secret is.
PRESETSLook-alikes. Left swaps the friends drawn after TV and film characters for original ones with the same sounds. Right brings them back.

To leave Rainbow mode for the full Felucca synth, hold HOME and SAVE together for 3 seconds. Your last Felucca session is right where you left it, and Rainbow mode comes back at the next power-on.

The panel

ControlWhat it does
KeysPlay the friend. The note's letter shows big in its color, the friend hops, and a staff at the top writes the note.
PRESETSThe next or previous friend (94 of them, and a secret one). Each has its own sky and favorite beat.
ALGORITHMHow the keys play (below).
SELECTThe beat slower or faster: SLOW, WALK, FAST.
KNOB 1Big and small. Low notes make a big friend, high notes a small one.
KNOB 2Day to night. The sky darkens and the sound gets softer.
KNOB 3Echo. Copies of the friend follow the sound around.
KNOB 4Wiggle. The sound wobbles, and so does the friend.
OCT- / OCT+The keys an octave down or up (the friend grows or shrinks, as with KNOB 1).
FXHICCUP: the sound stutters.
SCLBACKWARDS: the sound plays in reverse.
ENVSLEEPY: each note winds down like a record player. It wakes up by itself after 3 seconds.
LFOSQUEAKY: everything an octave up.
EDITGIANT: everything an octave down.
GLOFREEZE: the sound freezes and the friend turns to ice.
The top row's tricks go on with a tap and off with another, one at a time. The lit button shows which.
PLAYStarts and stops the beat: drums and a bass line that always fits.
SEQThe next beat, of 21 (DANCE, MARCH, SPOOKY, ROCK, DISCO and on to BREAK).
ARPSparkle: a held key plays up and down by itself.
RECThe next sky, with confetti.
HOMEA tap: a surprise friend. Held 1 second: the grown-ups' settings (above).
SAVEWRITE, a big staff for writing a song (below).
MASTERVolume, up to the limit the grown-ups chose.

ALGORITHM: how the keys play

Turn ALGORITHM one click at a time. Each setting has a picture, for kids who don't read yet.

SettingWhat the keys do
1 FRIENDEach key plays its note.
3 FRIENDSEach key plays the major chord built on it, and three friends sing.
STRUMLike an Omnichord. A black key picks a chord; the white keys strum it, left to right.
ACCORDIONThe black keys hold chords while the white keys play the tune. The lower row plays plain chords, the next row jazz ninths. ARP here sparkles the tune, then everything, then nothing.
GUESSAn ear game. A friend sings a note; find it on the keys. PLAY hears it again.
FOLLOWSongs to learn. The next key lights up. SEQ picks the song, of 17.
CHORDSChord lessons. The next chord's keys light up; hold exactly those. SEQ picks the lesson, PLAY starts a slow lofi beat.
COPY MEA memory game on C, D, E, G and A. The friend plays a pattern, then it's your turn. PLAY hears it again.

SAVE: writing a song

SAVE opens a big staff, Mario Paint style. The song stays on the FM-1 after it's switched off.

In WRITEWhat it does
KeysWrite a note in the yellow column. The same key again takes it out. Two keys at once make a chord.
PRESETSThe friend to write with. A song can have three.
OCT- / OCT+The yellow column back and forward (32 columns, 4 pages).
HOMEWipes the column.
PLAYPlays the song in a loop. Keys pressed while it plays are added where the beat is.
SEQA beat under it.
SELECTSlower or faster.
REC held 2 sA new, empty song.
SAVEBack to playing.

Stars and secrets

Every game won is a star: a right GUESS, a COPY ME round, a FOLLOW song or a CHORDS lesson. Hold HOME for a second, then turn KNOB 3, to see how close the next secret is.

StarsWhat opens
10The STAR hat. It joins the round on HOME held, then KNOB 4.
25The northern lights, a secret sky. It joins REC's round.
50GOLD DUCKY, a secret friend. It joins PRESETS.

The drum friends (Scissors, Beat Bot, Toy Drum, Bongo and Monkey) play a different drum on every key, and the band shows its name instead of a letter.
