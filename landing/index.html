Rainbow mode

A music toy for little hands. Free firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1 synthesizer, made for young children.

Try it in your browser Put it on an FM-1

For now, the FM-1 installer has the previous version while the newest one is checked. The browser version has everything new.

No FM-1 needed to try it. Press the keys on the screen, or use your computer keyboard.

Download the video (70 seconds) to share it.

What it feels like

  • Every key shows its note, big, in its own color, like the colored bells in a music class. C is red, D orange, E yellow, F green, G teal, A purple, B pink.
  • 94 friends. Each has its own picture, sound, sky and favorite beat: ducks, an axolotl, a unicorn, a ghost, a rocket, a bunny and more. Six of them (a T-Rex, a skeleton, a rancher, a rodeo friend, a vacuum and a space hero) were suggested by u/veecheech on r/MVaveFM1, six play drum kits, every key a different drum, and eight have the classic sounds of the Prophet-5 synth: an elephant's brass, a race car's sync sweep, a jellyfish's strings, a bear's bass, a snowman's bells, a crab's clav, a wolf's howl and a camel's lead. Three more (a scientist, a flytrap and a sunflower) squelch like a 303, for the acid fans. Twelve came from a child's own ideas: Spider Mom, Dragon Duck, Spider Grandma, a night light, a green queen, an axolotl king, a fairy monster, a morning glory, a grouch in a can, a tiger cub, a raccoon with a box of treasures, and goldenrod. Eighteen more come in sets: the farm, the sea, bugs, dinosaurs, food and things that go. And a few secrets wait for stars.
  • 21 beats with a bass line that always fits the keys. Dance, March, Spooky, Rock, Disco, Hip Hop, Train, Samba, Reggae, Lullaby, Hoedown, Lofi, Acid, Polka, Bossa, Funk, Ska, Trap, Salsa, Shuffle and a real drum break.
  • Every knob does something you can see and hear: big and small, day and night, echo, wiggle, and a choir of three friends.
  • Six sound tricks on the top buttons, one tap on and one tap off: hiccup, backwards, sleepy, squeaky, giant and freeze.
  • A dial for how the keys play. ALGORITHM turns from one friend to a choir, an Omnichord-style STRUM, an ACCORDION (black keys play chords, plain on the low row and jazz ninths on the next, white keys the tune), a gentle ear game (GUESS), FOLLOW, seventeen old songs to learn with the next key lit, CHORDS, lessons from first chords to lofi with the chord's keys lit and named (an idea from u/Yablan), and COPY ME, a memory game where the friend plays a pattern, the child plays it back and it grows by a note each round. Each has a picture, for children who do not read yet. Keys held together show the chord's letters and name.
  • Write your own songs. SAVE opens a big staff: each key puts a friend on it as a note, PLAY plays the song back while the friends hop along, and it is still there tomorrow (an idea from u/lastapoc, after Mario Paint).
  • A music staff. A treble clef drops in with each note played and writes it as a colored head, chords stacked, so you can see where the sounds live. How long a key is held sets the note: a quick tap is an eighth or a sixteenth, a long press a half or a whole note (an idea from u/theskyisfalling1).
  • Fifteen skies (rainbow, stars, hearts, bubbles, flowers, confetti, clouds, snow, autumn leaves, a desert, a street where all the monster friends live, under the sea, rain, fireworks, and a secret one), a hello that greets anyone by name (set it right on the FM-1), a surprise friend on HOME, ten hats any friend can wear (hold HOME, turn KNOB 4), and a volume limit for small ears that grown-ups set: eight steps, kept when it is switched off.
  • Look-alikes, your call. A few friends are drawn after TV and film characters. Hold HOME for a second and turn PRESETS left to swap them for original stand-ins with the same sounds (a spider, a fox, a bat, an alien and more), right to bring them back (an idea from u/ReallyLongLake).

Try it

In your browser

The same firmware, running on your screen. Works on computers, phones and tablets.

Open the demo It needs sound on, and you may have to tap once to start.

On an M-VAVE FM-1

Connect it by USB and press Install. It takes about a minute.

Open the installer Needs Chrome or Edge on a computer or an Android phone, and a USB cable. Safari and iPhones cannot do it.

Is it safe? Can I go back?

You can return to the official firmware at any time: the installer page has a "Return to official V15" section. You download the official file from M-VAVE's website, and the installer backs up the FM-1 before it goes back. Installing firmware is at your own risk. This is an independent fan project and is not made or endorsed by M-VAVE.

How do I leave Rainbow mode?

It starts every time the FM-1 is switched on. Hold HOME and SAVE together for 3 seconds to get the full Felucca synthesizer, with your last session restored. Switch the FM-1 off and on to come back to Rainbow mode.

Built on Felucca, the multi-engine synthesizer firmware for the FM-1 by Leo Kuroshita and Hügelton Instruments. Rainbow mode is a fork of it and adds a toy mode on top: github.com/hugelton/Felucca.
