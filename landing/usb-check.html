FM-1 USB check

For an FM-1 whose screen stays black after an interrupted install. This page shows what the FM-1 tells the phone or computer over USB, so you know which way back applies. It only reads: nothing is sent to the FM-1 and nothing is written.

  1. Use Chrome (Android, Windows, macOS, Linux, ChromeOS) or Edge.
  2. On a phone, connect the FM-1 through an OTG adapter (USB-C plug to USB-A socket) and a USB-A to USB-C data cable. Switch the FM-1 on and wait 10 seconds.
  3. Press Look for USB devices and pick the FM-1 in the list (it may be called "Unknown device"). If the list shows nothing, unplug it, plug it in again and press the button again.
