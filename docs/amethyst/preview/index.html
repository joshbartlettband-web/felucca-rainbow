How to play it

The break starts on its own. Hold a low key for the bass and a high key for a chord; every white key is in A minor, so nothing clashes. Tap the black keys to chop the break.

Hold BUILD for a bar or two, then let go. The drop lands on the next bar, the lasers come on, and the crowd calls for a rewind: lighters up, REWIND flashing. Answer it. Three rewinds on a stage cut its VIP.

The longer you play, the further the moss climbs. MOSS speeds it up and darkens the sound.

What this is

A sketch of Amethyst's design in a web page, so you can hear and see the idea before any firmware exists. The sound is made by the browser, not by Melodee's engines, and the drums are synthesized here, so it only hints at the real thing. The breaks are our own patterns in the old style; no recordings are sampled.

The screen is the FM-1's 240 by 240. The stage pictures use 16 colors each; the moss, lasers and frame are drawn over them live.
