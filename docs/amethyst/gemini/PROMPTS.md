# Pictures made with Gemini

Made with Google's Gemini image model, `gemini-3-pro-image` (Nano Banana Pro), on November 5, 2026, by
`tools/amethyst/gemini_image.py`, then scaled to 720 x 720. They're sketches for Amethyst's stages
(`docs/AMETHYST.md`, section 6), used by `tools/amethyst/layers.py`.

Four are changes of Commons photos. They keep the photographer's credit and license, and say they were changed by an
AI. Three are layers on black, made from words alone. Pictures made only by an AI may not be anyone's copyright
(in the US, at least, that's how it stands); we treat them as free to use and always say where they came from.

Every change of a photo ended with: "Keep the buildings' shapes, windows and doors exactly where they are. No text, no
signs, no logos, no people. A real photograph, moody and cinematic, square crop."
